# Hospital Visitor Access Policy

Document ID: HVA-COMPACT
Version: 1.0
Owner: Hospital Operations
Fictional demonstration document

## Purpose and context

This document describes visitor access arrangements for inpatient wards. It was prepared after discussions with Security and ward staff. The information supports a welcoming and orderly hospital environment.

## Policy rules

### 1. General visiting hours

General visitors must leave inpatient wards by 8:00 p.m. daily, unless the nurse in charge authorizes an exception.

### 2. Visitor registration

Before allowing a general visitor beyond the security desk, Security staff must verify one government-issued photo ID and record the visitor's name, destination, and arrival time.

### 3. Visitor badges

General visitors must wear the issued visitor badge above the waist while inside the hospital and return it when leaving.

## Explanatory note

A visitor arriving shortly before closing may hear a closing-time reminder at the security desk. This is an example of a conversation, not an additional policy requirement.
